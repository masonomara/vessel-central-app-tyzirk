
# Notification Settings - User Guide

## Overview

Vessel Central gives you complete control over your notifications. You can customize which notifications you receive, when you receive them, and how they're delivered.

## Accessing Notification Settings

1. Open the app
2. Go to the **Profile** tab
3. Tap on **Notifications** under Settings
4. Customize your preferences

## Notification Settings

### Master Toggle

Turn all notifications on or off with a single switch. When disabled, you won't receive any notifications from the app.

### Notification Frequency

Choose how often you want to receive notifications:

- **Realtime**: Get notifications immediately as events happen (recommended)
- **Hourly**: Receive a summary of notifications once per hour
- **Daily**: Get a daily digest of all notifications

### Quiet Hours

Set specific times when you don't want to be disturbed:

1. Enable **Quiet Hours**
2. Set your **Start Time** (e.g., 10:00 PM)
3. Set your **End Time** (e.g., 8:00 AM)

During quiet hours:
- Push notifications are muted
- Sounds and vibrations are disabled
- In-app notifications still appear (but silently)

### Notification Categories

Customize each type of notification independently:

#### 1. Maintenance
Notifications about scheduled maintenance tasks and reminders.

**Examples:**
- "Oil change is due in 3 days"
- "Engine maintenance completed"

#### 2. Issues
Alerts about new issues and status updates.

**Examples:**
- "New issue reported: Engine overheating"
- "Issue resolved: Electrical problem"

#### 3. Supplies
Updates about supply requests and inventory.

**Examples:**
- "New supply request: Engine oil"
- "Your supply request has been approved"

#### 4. Documents
Notifications about new documents and uploads.

**Examples:**
- "New document uploaded: Insurance Policy.pdf"
- "Document expiring soon: Safety Certificate"

#### 5. Tasks
Alerts about task assignments and completions.

**Examples:**
- "New task assigned: Clean deck"
- "Task completed: Inventory check"

#### 6. Approvals
Notifications about approval requests and decisions.

**Examples:**
- "Your request has been approved"
- "New approval request pending"

#### 7. System
System updates and important announcements.

**Examples:**
- "System maintenance scheduled"
- "New app version available"

### Per-Category Settings

For each category, you can control:

- **Enable/Disable**: Turn this category on or off
- **Push Notifications**: Receive push notifications on your device
- **In-App Notifications**: See updates in the activity feed
- **Sound**: Play a sound when notifications arrive
- **Vibration**: Vibrate when notifications arrive

## Activity Feed

View all your recent notifications in the Activity Feed:

1. Look for the bell icon with a badge showing unread count
2. Tap on any notification to mark it as read
3. Tap "Mark all read" to clear all unread notifications
4. Pull down to refresh and see new notifications

### Understanding Notification Icons

Each notification type has a unique icon and color:

- 🔧 **Maintenance** - Orange wrench icon
- ⚠️ **Issues** - Red warning icon
- 📦 **Supplies** - Blue box icon
- 📄 **Documents** - Blue document icon
- ✓ **Tasks** - Purple checkmark icon
- 👍 **Approvals** - Purple thumbs up icon
- 🔔 **System** - Gray bell icon

## Tips for Better Notification Management

### Reduce Notification Overload

1. **Disable less important categories**: Turn off notifications for categories you don't need
2. **Use hourly or daily frequency**: Batch notifications instead of receiving them in real-time
3. **Enable quiet hours**: Set times when you don't want to be disturbed
4. **Turn off sounds**: Keep push notifications but disable sounds for less urgent categories

### Stay Informed Without Interruptions

**Recommended Settings for Owners:**
- Enable: Approvals, Issues, System
- Disable sounds: Documents, Supplies
- Quiet hours: 10 PM - 8 AM

**Recommended Settings for Managers:**
- Enable: All categories
- Disable sounds: Documents
- Quiet hours: 11 PM - 7 AM

**Recommended Settings for Crew:**
- Enable: Tasks, Issues, Maintenance
- Disable: Approvals, System
- Quiet hours: 10 PM - 8 AM

### Customize for Your Workflow

- **On duty**: Enable all notifications with sounds
- **Off duty**: Enable quiet hours or reduce frequency
- **Vacation**: Disable all notifications or set to daily digest

## Notification Behavior

### Push Notifications

- Appear on your lock screen and notification center
- Can be tapped to open the relevant screen in the app
- Show a badge count on the app icon
- Respect your device's notification settings

### In-App Notifications

- Always visible in the activity feed
- Show unread count badge
- Can be marked as read individually or all at once
- Persist even if push notifications are disabled

## Troubleshooting

### Not receiving notifications?

1. Check if notifications are enabled in the app settings
2. Verify the specific category is enabled
3. Check if you're in quiet hours
4. Ensure your device allows notifications for Vessel Central
5. Check your device's Do Not Disturb settings

### Too many notifications?

1. Adjust notification frequency to hourly or daily
2. Disable categories you don't need
3. Turn off sounds and vibrations
4. Enable quiet hours for specific times

### Notifications delayed?

1. Check your internet connection
2. Ensure the app is running in the background
3. Verify notification frequency setting
4. Try pulling down to refresh the activity feed

## Privacy & Data

- Notification preferences are stored locally on your device
- Your settings are not shared with other users
- You can reset to default settings at any time
- Clearing app data will reset your preferences

## Reset to Defaults

If you want to start fresh:

1. Open Notification Settings
2. Scroll to the bottom
3. Tap "Reset to Defaults"
4. Confirm the action

This will restore all notification settings to their original values.

## Need Help?

If you're having trouble with notifications:

1. Try resetting to defaults
2. Check your device's notification permissions
3. Restart the app
4. Contact support if issues persist

## Summary

The notification system is designed to keep you informed without overwhelming you. Take a few minutes to customize your settings based on your role and preferences. You can always adjust them later as your needs change.

**Remember**: You're in control! The app will respect your preferences and only send notifications you've chosen to receive.
