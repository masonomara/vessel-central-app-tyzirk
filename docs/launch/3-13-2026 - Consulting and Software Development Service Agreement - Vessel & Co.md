**2\.	SCOPE OF WORK**

2.1	*Project Overview.* The Agency shall provide mobile application refinement and App Store submission services for the Client's yacht management demonstration application, known as Vessel Central. Vessel Central is a mobile application prototype built to demonstrate a shared operational platform for yacht owners, fleet managers, and crew — coordinating maintenance, scheduling, documents, and expenses across a managed fleet. The existing prototype contains role-based dashboards, task management flows, document views, calendar screens, and analytics displays, all populated with demonstration data. The Agency's scope is limited to refining this prototype into a polished, crash-free demonstration suitable for App Store distribution and in-person presentation at industry events.

2.2	*Project Objectives.* The primary objectives of the agreement are to:

(a) Restyle the existing Vessel Central prototype to reflect the Client's brand identity

(b) Resolve all crashes, dead-end screens, and navigation failures so the application performs reliably during in-person demonstrations

(c) Replace placeholder and developer-quality mock data with realistic maritime content suitable for a professional audience

(d) Submit the application to the Apple App Store and Google Play Store with all required assets, metadata, and compliance materials

2.3	*Project Services.* The services that will be performed by Agency are organized into four phases totaling thirty-four (34) hours:

(a) *UI Cleanup & Styling (15 hours, Week 1).* Restyle the application to match the Vessel Central brand. Apply brand color palette. Remove all visual indicators of prototype status, including demo badges, placeholder phone numbers, and generic vessel names. Ensure every screen appears intentional and professionally finished.

(b) *Crash Fixes & Screen Completion (10 hours, Week 2).* Repair the document viewer, which currently references non-existent files. Complete thin or dead-end detail screens for tasks and issues. Conduct a full walkthrough on physical iOS and Android devices covering every tab, modal, and form. Confirm layouts hold across screen sizes and the experience is smooth.

(c) *Demo Data & Placeholder Removal (4 hours, Week 3).* Replace mock data with realistic vessel names, maintenance logs, and maritime terminology. Seed enough content that the app feels populated. Remove hardcoded profile data. Incorporate multiple professional views — owner, crew, and broker — suitable for the Client's launch event demo stations.

(d) *App Store Submission (5 hours, Week 4).* Create app icons, screenshots, and metadata. Draft privacy policy. Submit to the Apple App Store and Google Play Store. Includes one round of review feedback if initially rejected.

2.4	*Scope Definition and Prioritization.* The Agency and Client will communicate regularly during the engagement to review progress and address questions. The scope of work is defined in this Article 2\. Any changes or additions will be documented and subject to the change order process in Article 4\.

2.5	*Workload Adjustment.* If the Client determines that the agreed project scope is insufficient to meet the evolving demands of Vessel Central's development, both parties will reassess the terms. Adjustments to the scope will be made through formal change orders to ensure the project continues smoothly without delays or unmet priorities.

2.6	*Project Timeline.* Agency will use commercially reasonable efforts to complete the Services within a four (4) week project duration beginning on the Commencement Date, with a target submission to the Apple App Store and Google Play Store by the end of Week 4\. App Store review and approval timelines are outside Agency's control. Client acknowledges that Apple and Google review processes can take four to five (4-5) business days or longer, and that rejection may require rework and resubmission. All timelines are subject to Client's timely performance of obligations and approval requirements as specified herein.

2.7	*Technical Specifications.* Agency will deliver a mobile application that includes:

	(a) iOS application compatible with iOS 16 and later

	(b) Android application compatible with Android 10 (API level 29\) and later

	(c) Cross-platform deployment from a single codebase built using React Native and Expo

	(d) Demonstration data only — no backend database, no API integrations, no user authentication, no persistent data storage

	The application is a demonstration product containing pre-populated sample data. It does not connect to any backend services, does not store or transmit user data, and does not provide functional vessel management capabilities.

2.8	*Success Criteria.* Agency will exercise commercially reasonable efforts to deliver:

	(a) A mobile application free from crashes during standard navigation and walkthrough of all screens

	(b) Professional visual presentation consistent with Client's brand identity

	(c) Realistic demonstration data suitable for presentation to yacht owners, crew, and brokers

	(d) Successful submission to the Apple App Store and Google Play Store, with approval subject to platform review

2.9	*Communication Hours and Protocol.* The Agency's standard communication hours are from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST. 2.10	*Demonstration Scope Limitation.* The Deliverables under this Agreement are limited to a demonstration mobile application. The application will contain pre-populated sample data and will not include:

(a) Backend database or server infrastructure

	(b) User authentication or account management

	(c) Persistent data storage or data synchronization

	(d) Payment processing or billing integration

	(e) Real-time communication or push notifications

	(f) Document upload, storage, or retrieval functionality
