# Vessel & Co. Support

## About the App

Vessel & Co. is a yacht fleet management app for owners, managers, and crew. It helps you track vessels, maintenance tasks, issues, supply requests, documents, certifications, charters, equipment, contacts, and scheduling -- all from one place.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I get started?

Sign in with your email and password, or use one of the demo roles (Owner, Manager, Crew) to explore the app with sample data.

### How do I switch between roles?

Log out from the profile menu, then sign in with a different account or select a different demo role.

### Where is my data stored?

All data is stored locally on your device. Nothing is sent to external servers. Your data stays on your phone or tablet.

### How do I delete my data?

You can delete all app data by:
- **iOS:** Go to Settings > General > iPhone Storage > Vessel & Co. > Delete App
- **Android:** Go to Settings > Apps > Vessel & Co. > Storage > Clear Data

Uninstalling the app also removes all stored data.

### What permissions does the app need?

The app requests permissions only when you use features that require them:
- **Camera** -- to photograph maintenance issues, equipment, or documents
- **Photo Library** -- to attach existing photos to records
- **Calendar** -- to sync events with your device calendar
- **Notifications** -- for task reminders and deadline alerts

You can manage permissions anytime in your device settings.

### How do I report a bug?

Email hello@vesselandco.yachts with a description of the issue, the device you're using, and screenshots if possible.
