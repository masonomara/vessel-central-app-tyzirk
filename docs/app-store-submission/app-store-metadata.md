## Description (4000 chars max)

Vessel & Co. is a comprehensive yacht fleet management app built for vessel owners, fleet managers, and crew members. Manage every aspect of your maritime operations from a single, intuitive interface.

Whether you oversee one yacht or an entire fleet, Vessel & Co. gives you the tools to stay organized, keep your vessels in top condition, and coordinate your team effectively.

ROLE-BASED DASHBOARDS

Every user gets a dashboard tailored to their role:

- Owners see fleet-wide KPIs, expense summaries, and operational status at a glance
- Managers track task assignments, upcoming deadlines, and resource allocation
- Crew members view their assignments, report issues, and log completed work

MAINTENANCE MANAGEMENT

Schedule recurring and one-time maintenance tasks. Assign work to crew, track costs, attach photos and documents, and maintain a complete history of every service performed on every vessel.

ISSUE TRACKING

Report and resolve problems quickly. Create issue reports with priority levels, assign them to crew, attach photos for documentation, and track resolution from open to closed.

SUPPLY REQUESTS

Streamline procurement with a built-in supply request workflow. Submit requests, track approval status (pending, approved, ordered, received, denied), and keep a clear paper trail.

DOCUMENT MANAGEMENT

Store and organize vessel documents -- registrations, insurance policies, inspection reports, and more. Track expiration dates so nothing lapses. Control access by role.

CREW CERTIFICATIONS

Monitor crew qualifications and certification expiry dates. Get alerts before certifications lapse so you can plan renewals in advance.

CHARTER LOGS

Record charter activity including dates, guest counts, itineraries, revenue, expenses, and broker information. Build a complete history of your charter operations.

EQUIPMENT INVENTORY

Catalog equipment across your fleet. Track condition, location, inspection dates, and maintenance history for every asset.

CALENDAR & SCHEDULING

Plan and coordinate events, maintenance windows, charter schedules, and crew rotations. Sync with your device calendar and set reminders.

CONTACTS DIRECTORY

Keep crew, vendors, marinas, emergency contacts, and owner contacts organized and accessible to the right people.

EXPENSE TRACKING

Log expenses with approval workflows. Owners and managers can review and approve costs to maintain financial oversight.

ACTIVITY LOGS

A complete audit trail of all actions taken in the app. Know who did what and when.

KEY FEATURES

- Works offline -- all data stored locally on your device
- No subscription required -- completely free
- Portrait and landscape support on iPad
- Push notifications for task assignments and deadline reminders
- Global search across all vessels, tasks, issues, and documents
- Clean, professional interface designed for maritime professionals

Vessel & Co. is built by Vessel and Co Yacht Management L.L.C., based in St. Petersburg, Florida.
