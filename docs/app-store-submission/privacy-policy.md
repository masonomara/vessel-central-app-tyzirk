This Privacy Policy describes how Vessel and Co Yacht Management L.L.C. ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Vessel & Co. mobile application ("the App").

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

When using the App, you may enter the following types of information:

- Account information (name, email address, role)
- Vessel details (name, type, registration, specifications)
- Maintenance records and task descriptions
- Issue reports and resolution notes
- Supply requests and inventory data
- Contact information for crew, vendors, and marinas (names, phone numbers, email addresses, company names)
- Calendar events and scheduling data
- Crew certification details (certification type, issue date, expiry date)
- Charter logs (dates, guest counts, itinerary, revenue, expenses)
- Equipment inventory (type, condition, inspection dates)
- Expense records
- Photos, videos, and documents you attach to records

### Information Collected Automatically

The App does not collect analytics, usage data, or telemetry. We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or similar technologies.

### Device Permissions

The App may request access to the following device features, only when you initiate an action that requires them:

- **Camera:** To capture photos of maintenance issues, equipment, and documents
- **Photo Library:** To attach existing images to records
- **Microphone:** To record audio when capturing video
- **Calendar:** To sync events with your device calendar
- **Reminders:** To set alerts for maintenance deadlines and certification expirations
- **Notifications:** To deliver task assignments, deadline reminders, and status updates

You can revoke any permission at any time through your device settings.

---

## 2. How Your Data Is Stored

All data you enter into the App is stored locally on your device. The App does not transmit your data to external servers operated by us. There is no cloud database, no remote backup, and no server-side storage of your information.

Data is stored using your device's local storage mechanism and persists until you uninstall the App or manually clear the App's data through your device settings.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party services, each of which may collect limited data as described below:

### Google Maps SDK (Android only)

Used to display maps within the App. The Google Maps SDK may collect approximate location data, device identifiers, and diagnostic information in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. This data is collected by Google, not by us. See: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### Apple Push Notification Service (iOS)

Used to deliver push notifications on iOS devices. Apple receives a device push token to route notifications. See: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

---

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your personal information with third parties. The only data shared with third parties is the limited technical data collected by the third-party services described in Section 3, which is governed by their respective privacy policies.

---

## 5. Data Security

Your data is stored locally on your device and is protected by your device's built-in security features (passcode, biometric lock, device encryption). We do not apply additional encryption to locally stored data beyond what your device operating system provides.

---

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained on your device for as long as the App is installed. You can delete all App data at any time by:

- Uninstalling the App from your device
- Clearing the App's data through your device settings (Settings > Apps > Vessel & Co. > Clear Data on Android, or offloading/deleting the app on iOS)

There is no account to delete because the App does not maintain server-side accounts.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the App, please contact us at the email address below.

## 8. Your Rights

### For All Users

You have the right to:

- Access all data stored by the App (visible directly in the App interface)
- Delete all data stored by the App (by clearing App data or uninstalling)
- Revoke device permissions at any time through your device settings

### For European Economic Area (EEA) Residents

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have additional rights including:

- **Right of Access:** You can view all data stored by the App directly in the App interface.
- **Right to Rectification:** You can edit any data you have entered through the App interface.
- **Right to Erasure:** You can delete all data by clearing App data or uninstalling.
- **Right to Data Portability:** Data is stored locally on your device and can be accessed through your device's file system.
- **Right to Object:** The App does not process data for marketing, profiling, or automated decision-making.

The legal basis for processing your data is your consent (you choose what data to enter) and legitimate interest (the App functions as a management tool for your use).
