## Full Description (4000 chars max)

Vessel & Co. is a comprehensive yacht fleet management app designed for vessel owners, fleet managers, and crew members. Manage maintenance, issues, supplies, documents, certifications, charters, equipment, contacts, expenses, and scheduling from a single app.

ROLE-BASED ACCESS

Each user gets a tailored experience:
- Yacht owners see fleet-wide KPIs, expense summaries, and vessel status
- Fleet managers track task assignments, deadlines, and resource allocation
- Crew members view assignments, report issues, and log completed work

YACHT MAINTENANCE MANAGEMENT

Schedule recurring and one-time maintenance tasks for your yacht fleet. Assign work to crew members, track costs, attach photos and documents, and maintain a complete service history for every vessel.

MARINE ISSUE TRACKING

Report and resolve problems quickly. Create issue reports with priority levels, assign them to crew, attach photos, and track resolution from open to closed. Keep a clear record of every problem and fix.

VESSEL SUPPLY REQUESTS

Streamline procurement with a supply request workflow. Submit requests, track approval status through pending, approved, ordered, received, or denied stages.

MARITIME DOCUMENT MANAGEMENT

Store and organize vessel documents including registrations, insurance policies, and inspection reports. Track expiration dates so nothing lapses. Control document access by role.

CREW CERTIFICATION TRACKING

Monitor crew qualifications and certification expiry dates. Receive alerts before certifications lapse so you can plan renewals and stay compliant.

YACHT CHARTER LOGS

Record charter activity with dates, guest counts, itineraries, revenue, expenses, and broker information. Build a complete history of charter operations.

BOAT EQUIPMENT INVENTORY

Catalog equipment across your fleet. Track condition, location, inspection dates, and maintenance history for every piece of equipment.

MARINE CALENDAR AND SCHEDULING

Plan and coordinate events, maintenance windows, charter schedules, and crew rotations. Sync with your device calendar and set reminders for deadlines.

FLEET CONTACTS DIRECTORY

Organize contacts for crew, vendors, marinas, emergency services, and vessel owners. Keep the right information accessible to the right people.

VESSEL EXPENSE TRACKING

Log expenses with approval workflows. Owners and managers can review and approve costs for complete financial oversight of fleet operations.

ACTIVITY AUDIT LOGS

Maintain a complete audit trail of all actions. Know who did what and when across your entire fleet management operation.

KEY FEATURES
- Works completely offline with all data stored locally on your device
- No subscription required and completely free to use
- Role-based dashboards for owners, managers, and crew
- Push notifications for task assignments and deadline reminders
- Global search across vessels, tasks, issues, documents, and contacts
- Professional interface designed for the maritime industry
- Supports tablets with landscape mode

Built by Vessel and Co Yacht Management L.L.C. in St. Petersburg, Florida.

Download Vessel & Co. today and take control of your yacht fleet management.
