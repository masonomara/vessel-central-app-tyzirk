Overview
New Roots is a regional food access nonprofit that partners with hospitals, governments, universities, and other nonprofits to build equitable food systems in under-resourced communities. Their signature model is Fresh Stop markets: biweekly popup markets in food deserts connecting local farmers (who receive upfront payment) with shareholders (community members paying $6–25/share on a sliding scale, SNAP accepted).
The model works. Shareholders get farm-fresh food and community connection. Farmers get guaranteed income. Sponsors learn to run Fresh Markets independently. New Roots no longer operates markets directly, they train and equip partners to help scale their mission.
2025 was their first major grant-seeking year; 2026 brings direct funding. To keep infrastructure pace with market growth, New Roots must unify brand and messaging across all channels, create professional documents for each audience (sponsors, shareholders, farmers, funders), rebuild their website to signal capability and tell their story, build a knowledge base for sponsors, and organize technical systems (CRM, app, messaging).
New Roots needs someone to lead strategy and design for digital infrastructure: brand and messaging, technical systems, and ongoing initiatives.
